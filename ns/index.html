Florilegium namespace

Namespace IRI: https://w3id.org/florilegium/ns#, conventionally abbreviated fl:.

Florilegium is a reading workspace. Its records are W3C Web Annotations on exact passages, and decisions about them, in JSON-LD. This page defines the terms of its own that those records use. Terms from PROV, Dublin Core, ActivityStreams and the Web Annotation vocabulary are used as those vocabularies define them.

  • https://w3id.org/florilegium/ns/v1.jsonld: the JSON-LD context, version 1
  • https://w3id.org/florilegium/ns/v1.ttl: this vocabulary in Turtle (OWL 2), version 1

A version of either file does not change once a record names it. New or changed terms arrive in a new version, so a record keeps the meaning it was written with. Version 1 is a draft until the first record that names it.

Terms, version 1

fl:lineage Property, a link: lineage
The lineage a revision belongs to: every revision of one annotation, or one decision, shares it. It groups revisions and says nothing else about how they relate.
fl:prev Property, a link: previous revision
The revision this one immediately replaces, in the same lineage and the same place. Narrower than prov:wasRevisionOf, which also holds between revisions further apart. Absent on a first revision. Narrower than http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#wasRevisionOf.
fl:place Property, a link: place
The place a record was written in: one person's, or one circle's, body of materials, records and decisions.
fl:protocol Property, a link: protocol
The exact protocol revision in force when the record was written, named by the hash of its bytes.
fl:state Property, a value: state
Whether its author still puts this annotation forward: active, or withdrawn. A withdrawal is itself a revision; it does not undo a decision.
fl:issue Property, a link: issue
The question an annotation answers, named by the lineage that raised it. Annotations on one issue are alternatives to one another. An annotation without it answers its own lineage.
fl:actingFor Property, a link: acting for
On an annotation made by an assistant: the person on whose behalf it was made. It relates the annotation to that person. In PROV terms the assistant acted on behalf of the person in making it; this property gives the assistant no authority to decide.
fl:figure Property, a value: figure
On a target that is a whole figure: the figure's identifier within its representation. A local name, meaningful only beside that representation.
fl:Decision Class: Decision
The record of a decision made under a place's protocol by someone authorized to make it. The record is an entity; the act of deciding that produced it is a separate activity, which this record does not name. Narrower than http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#Entity.
fl:decider Property, a link: decider
The person who made the decision, in the role the rule requires. Always a person; never an assistant.
fl:effects Property, a link: effects
What a decision does. All of a decision's effects take hold together, or none does.
fl:act Property, a value: act
What one effect does: adopt or reject an exact revision for a purpose, or, in a place's first decision, genesis.
fl:purpose Property, a value: purpose
The named purpose an effect decides for, as the protocol defines it. Standing for one purpose is not standing for another.
fl:subject Property, a link: subject
The exact revision something is about: the revision an effect adopts or rejects, an alternative considered, or the claim an evidence assessment assesses. Never a lineage and never 'the latest'.
fl:ownerActor Property, a link: owner
In a place's first decision: the actor who owns the place. An identity, not a name.
fl:expects Property, a link: expects
For each question a decision touches, the decision that stood on it when this one was made. A decision is refused if any has changed.
fl:key Property, a value: key
The question a decision stands on: an issue together with a purpose.
fl:head Property, a link: head
The decision that stood on a key, or none.
fl:rule Property, a value: rule
The protocol rule the decision was made under, such as owner. It records which rule applied; the software evaluates the rule before it writes the decision.
fl:members Property, a link: members
Who held the role the rule names at the moment of the decision.
fl:dissent Property, a link: dissent
Members who recorded disagreement with the decision.
fl:alternatives Property, a link: alternatives
The other active annotations on the same issue when the decision was made, each by its exact revision.
fl:dissented Property, a value: dissented
Whether an alternative's author dissented from the decision.
fl:rationale Property, a value: rationale
The decider's stated reason, in their own words. May be empty.
fl:display Property, a link: display
For each passage or figure a decision rests on, whether the decider's reader displayed it before the decision. It reports display by a reader, and claims neither reading nor understanding.
fl:representation Property, a link: representation
The exact representation in which something was observed.
fl:targetDigest Property, a value: target digest
The SHA-256 of the RFC 8785 form of an annotation's whole target value, binding an observation to that exact target.
fl:coverage Property, a value: coverage
How much of a target the reader displayed: full, partial or none; or unknown when nothing was observed.
fl:method Property, a value: method
The observation method and its version.
fl:observedAt Property, a value: observed at
When the observation that established the coverage was made. Absent when there was none.
fl:norm Class: Reading
A reading of a passage as stating a norm: that it obliges, permits or prohibits someone to do something. It is a claim about what the passage says. It does not impose the norm.
fl:modality Property, a value: modality
Whether the passage is read as an obligation, a permission or a prohibition.
fl:actor Property, a value: actor
Who the norm applies to, in the reader's words. Text, not an identity.
fl:action Property, a value: action
What the norm is about doing, in the reader's words.
fl:condition Property, a value: condition
When the norm applies, if the passage says.
fl:exceptions Property, a link: exceptions
The exceptions the passage states, in order.
fl:graph Class: Graph
A figure read as nodes and edges, such as a flowchart. A reading of the figure, saved beside it.
fl:dataset Class: Dataset
A figure read as series of values, such as a chart, each value with how it was obtained.
fl:nodes Property, a value: nodes
A graph's nodes, kept as written.
fl:edges Property, a value: edges
A graph's edges, kept as written.
fl:series Property, a value: series
The names of a dataset's series.
fl:unit Property, a value: unit
A dataset's unit as the figure gives it, or that it is unknown and why.
fl:values Property, a value: values
A dataset's values, each with its method: read, estimated or calculated.
fl:notes Property, a value: notes
Remarks on a structure, such as what could not be read.
fl:claim Class: Claim
A statement being examined, on the passage from which it was drawn. Saving it does not endorse the source or establish the statement.
fl:statement Property, a value: statement
What is claimed, in one sentence.
fl:uncertainty Property, a value: uncertainty
What is not known or not settled about it. Required, so that it is never left unsaid.
fl:evidence Class: Evidence assessment
An attributed assessment of how one source passage bears on one exact saved claim revision. The assessment is its author's; it establishes nothing by itself.
fl:relation Property, a value: relation
How the passage bears on the claim: supports, challenges or qualifies.
fl:reason Property, a value: reason
Why the assessor says so.
fl:limitations Property, a value: limitations
What limits the assessment.
fl:commitment Class: Commitment
A source-reported undertaking by a named party, on the passage that reports it. Keeping or adopting it does not make it a promise by anyone here, or record that it was kept.
fl:owner Property, a value: owner
Who the source says undertook it, as a name, or unknown. Text, never an actor identity.
fl:item Property, a value: item
What was undertaken.
fl:due Property, a value: due
The date the source gives, if it gives one as a date.
fl:timing Property, a value: timing
When, in the source's own words.
